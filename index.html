Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

❧
Can we bring our children?

While we love your little ones, we have decided to keep our wedding day an adults-only occasion. We hope this gives everyone a chance to let their hair down and enjoy a relaxing night off to celebrate with us!

Is there a gift registry?

Your presence at our wedding is the absolute best gift we could ask for! As we're already lucky enough to have a home filled with everything we need, we won't be having a traditional registry. However, if you would still like to honor us with a gift, a contribution toward our honeymoon fund would mean the world to us.

What should I wear?

Our venue has a cozy, historic vibe, so we're aiming for a Cocktail dress code (think cocktail dresses, jumpsuits, or dress trousers with a collared shirt or blazer).

Can I bring a guest?

Due to venue capacity, we can only accommodate guests who the invitation were sent to directly. We appreciate your understanding!

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Please let us know when you RSVP, and we'll do our best to accommodate your needs.

What time should I arrive?

We recommend arriving 20–30 minutes before the ceremony begins so you have plenty of time to get settled.
